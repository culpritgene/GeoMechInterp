# Toward language: a ladder of grammar experiments for SAE vs spline probes

**Framing.** Today's results split cleanly. In input space (Experiment 1) a cubic-spline net classifies the curved torus-product surfaces at 98% with ~460 parameters while ReLU/SAE families need one to two orders of magnitude more, with the gap concentrated at the curved surface. In activation space (Experiment 2) the gap almost vanishes: the path transformers store position as a distributed, hinge-friendly code (100-155 of 256 dims for 90% variance), the ring angle is a linear circle at the embedding and a filled disc afterwards, splines beat hinges by at most 0.1 R² on smooth targets and lose on decision-like ones, and the top-k SAE trails both by ~10x in parameters. The only genuinely non-linear codes we found were (i) position near link contacts, where two surfaces must be told apart, and (ii) the compressed d=64 models, whose position code went non-linear without widening the spline-vs-hinge margin. The lesson, sharpened by the critics of every proposal below, is that the next experiments must *force* a curved, product-structured or multi-dimensional feature to exist in the residual (not merely define one in the labels), verify its presence and non-linearity before any family comparison, subtract the probe-side expressivity gap with a synthetic-linear null, and only then ask whether cubic readouts beat first-order dictionaries. Every rung below is built that way, and each moves one step from geodesics toward the structures that language-like reasoning uses: counters, group composition, stacks and binding, hierarchy, nested scopes with agreement, multi-hop relational deduction, and in-context inference about rules.

## Shared protocol (applies to every rung)

- **Trainer.** One generic Task-object trainer derived from `train_path.py`: boolean per-token loss mask, plateau stopping (no improvement of the task metric for N evals, minimum step count), a `--loss_all_tokens` flag and an oracle-KL evaluator where the task has an exact predictor. `model.py` gains a `resid_mid` (post-attention, pre-MLP) and MLP-hidden hook, a rank-k factorised-embedding flag and a NoPE/RoPE flag. Data and checkpoints under `/var/tmp`.
- **Probes.** `probe.py` gains plateau stopping (val patience 1k steps, cap 10k, two learning rates), a cross-entropy variant of every family, a degree-1 spline probe with the same knots as the cubic one, a 2-layer ReLU MLP at matched parameters as the control for `spline2L`, removal of the tanh squash from `SplineProbe`, per-dimension R², and budgets recomputed per target including the output layer. Never probe answer or query positions when embeddings are tied.
- **Null control.** For every headline target, embed the exact ground-truth latent by a random orthogonal map into R^d with ~40 nuisance dimensions and matched noise, and run the identical probe grid. This is the ceiling of the spline-vs-hinge gap the target can ever show; transformer results are reported as excess over it, and targets whose ceiling ratio is below 3x are demoted to "probe-capacity controls".
- **Preconditions before any family comparison.** Presence (best supervised probe R² >= 0.9) and non-linearity (ridge <= 0.7 in every coordinate system tried) in a pre-registered (layer, position, stratum) cell with >= 20k tokens; 3 probe seeds at the deciding budgets; effect sizes reported relative to the seed SD.
- **SAE analysis module.** `sae_tuning.py` does not exist and must be written once (~200 lines): feature tuning maps over exact latent grids (circle, torus, simplex, tree), arc/tile detection, conjunction fraction, absorption across dictionary widths, irrep/subspace alignment, and dictionary size at fixed EV as a function of width and k.

## Rung 1 — Winding-class geodesics: circle x counter on the universal cover (distance to language: 2)

**Generator and tokenisation.** Existing `single_ring` cell graph (depth-5 oct-tree, ~1.9k cells) and the exact ring/tube angles from the fitted tori. Replace per-cell letters by an oriented-wall cocycle: one meridian cut (~60 directed cell edges at theta = 0) whose crossings count +1/-1, so wiggles cancel and the state is the homotopy class. Classes are defined relative to the free geodesic (extra ring windings k in {-1,0,1}; a tube-wall variant with l in {-2..2}), exact via Dijkstra on the cover graph (cell, k) = Kronecker product of the cell adjacency with the winding lattice. Measured: binds on 64% of triples, no 2-cycles, path length median 47 cells, p95 85, block ~140. Format: `BOS s SEP g SEP k SEP w1..wL EOS`, loss on path tokens, 300k paths. Splits by (s, g) cell pair and by (s, g, k) triple (a 20-line fix to `gen_paths.py`), plus 10% goal-region and start-region hold-outs. Tube-only first (18-hop laps, uniform in theta), ring-only second, `chain_link_two` with one wall per link (Z_3 x Z_3) third.

**Language/reasoning aspect.** A global invariant that no local step reveals, carried across the whole sequence: the analogue of tracking parity or nesting depth across a discourse. Honestly labelled: a 3- or 5-state counter attached to a continuous position, not a language above context-free.

**Ground-truth feature.** The unrolled angle Theta = theta + 2 pi k_t (theta in [0, 2 pi), cut at 0), a helix. Chart-free targets replace scalar Theta: T1 = (cos theta, sin theta) (+) one-hot k_t; T2 = remaining cover displacement dTheta (continuous, decides the step); T3 = the slow circle (cos(Theta/3), sin(Theta/3)) that embeds the cover without a branch cut. Why it might be non-linear: under a spiral code (lap modulates the disc radius seen today) Theta is a norm-like feature; under a direct sum it is linear and the only non-linearity is atan2. That artefact is already measured on the existing free-geodesic model (scalar theta, layer 0: ridge 0.82, ReLU m=4 0.975, spline1L m=4 0.994; layer 2: 0.61 / 0.85 / 0.87) and is the baseline any winding-model gap must exceed. Linear null on Theta computed by Monte Carlo from (cos, sin, k) on the actual token distribution (~0.90, not Var(2 pi k)/Var(Theta)).

**Training task and quality bar.** 6L d=256, 4L d=128, 3L d=64; plateau on validation success (min 30k/60k steps). Bar on 2k held-out triples: valid, reached and exact class (crossings recomputed) >= 98%; length <= 1.05x class optimum (1.02 reported); all-class sensitivity on 500 held-out (s, g) >= 98% per class; region hold-outs >= 95%.

**Probes and SAE-specific checks.** Layers {0, 2, 4, 6}; strata: within phi bins (57-70% of tokens on k != 0 paths sit in the inner-equator band that holds 19% of cells), within class before vs after the crossing (rules out reading the prompt token), near-wall vs far. Families incl. degree-1 spline and MLP2. Causal gate: patch the linear k direction mid-path and check the emitted class flips. SAE: firing maps over (theta sector x k); sector-only vs sector x lap tiles; dictionary size at EV 0.9 vs the free-task SAE at the same layer; absorption of the lap feature into sector features.

**Predictions and falsification.** Expected: linear CE on k >= 99%, ridge on T1 >= 0.95, MLP2 ties spline2L on Theta and the Theta gap equals the free-model chart gap: nothing curved, and the deliverable is the SAE factorisation count (tiles scaling with sheets vs shared sectors). Confirms: ridge on T1/T2 < 0.8 mid-layer while spline1L reaches >= 0.9 at <= 2.5k params, hinges and MLP2 >= 0.05 lower, SAE + ridge needs >= 10x, and the excess over the free-model gap is >= 0.1. Refutes: hinges within 0.02 of splines on a verified non-linear T2.

**Reuse and new code.** `gen_paths.py`, torus fits, `probe_strata.local_geometry`, `paths_data.py` (class tokens, crossing check), `model.py`, `train_path.py`, `probe.py`, `viz_resid.py`, `summarize_probes.py`. New: wall labeller + cover Dijkstra + pair split (~150 lines), cover targets and strata (~80).

**Effort and risks.** 4-5 days, ~30 GPU-hours. Risks: small model missing 1.05x (report smallest passing size); step-direction ties along the inner band; SCAN-style instruction stage deferred.

## Rung 2 — Group state tracking on Cayley walks: D_36, a flat torus and Z_360 under compression (distance to language: 2)

**Generator and tokenisation.** Three groups only, tables built from explicit representations and verified against sympy (closure order, rho(g)rho(h) = rho(gh) on the whole table): D_36 (order 72; generators r^{+-1..+-3}, s, sr; p(s) = 0.2; joint and factored output), Z_36 x Z_12 (the algebraic twin of `single_ring`), and Z_360 at d=32 (|G| >> dictionary width, so arcs on a smooth code are forced), plus Z_n for n in {12, 60, 360} for an arc-scaling law. FINAL-ONLY format is primary: `BOS e0 SEP r1..rk SEP ek EOS`, k in 1..12 (test 16 with the NoPE/RoPE flag), no answers in context. A QRY variant keeps a separate target array so answers are never fed back; checkpoint gap L_gap in {8, 16, 32} is a design variable. Tests: 20k fresh words; held-out adjacent generator bigrams/trigrams (replaces held-out start elements, which leak through tied embeddings); non-commutativity minimal pairs; shifted generator distribution.

**Language/reasoning aspect.** A word denotes an object only through sequential composition (entity state after swaps, multi-hop relation chains); non-commutativity makes order matter; s acts as a scope/negation operator. Stated honestly: "irreps are linear" replicates Chughtai et al. 2023 / Stander et al. 2023; the novel claims are the flip bit in the joint-output D_36 model and partial products in progress at the final position.

**Ground-truth feature.** The prefix product in exact irrep coordinates (D_36: (c, s, eps c, eps s) at frequencies 1..3; torus: two Fourier circles). Genuinely non-linear candidates: the flip bit eps as a quadratic form of two orthogonal circles (but four hinges compute |x1+x3| + |x2+x4|, so eps survives only if the null control gives a spline/hinge ceiling >= 3x); the lowest Fourier harmonic cos(k theta) not linearly present (a Chebyshev polynomial of the stored direction: one cubic-spline direction decodes k=3 exactly, hinges need ~10); partial products of contiguous subwords at the final position, per layer and at `resid_mid`/MLP-hidden, the only latent, non-output state the design forces. Mixed torus modes are a probe-capacity control, never a model feature.

**Training task and quality bar.** d in {32, 64, 256} x 4 layers, 3 seeds, plateau on validation accuracy (min 20k steps), 5-25 min per run, ~36 runs. Bar: >= 99.9% exact final element on 20k fresh words; >= 99.5% on held-out bigram/trigram, shifted-distribution and non-commutativity sets; k=16 >= 99% (D_36, torus); >= 90% of class-mean variance inside the irrep span.

**Probes and SAE-specific checks.** Null control first (0.5 day). Generator positions >= 2 tokens past the last state token, or the final position; QRY and answer positions excluded (linear by unembedding). Targets: irrep coordinates at all frequencies, element (CE), eps (logistic), the unused harmonic, partial products per interval. SAE grid of 8 (m, k) pairs with k >= irrep dimension so half-axis codes are possible; a matched-parameter unsupervised spline autoencoder (KANLayer encoder, linear decoder, MSE + L1) as the fair unsupervised comparison. Arc-scaling law: m at EV 0.9 vs n.

**Predictions and falsification.** Pre-registered cell: D_36 joint, d=64, generator positions, first layer with ridge >= 0.95 on the irrep entries. Confirms: logistic eps < 60% (two circles) and the smallest hinge probe at 99% eps needs > 2x the spline's parameters, SAE m <= 16 fails; the unused harmonic is read by spline1L m=2 at R² >= 0.9 while hinges need m >= 8, beyond the null-control ratio; Z_360 arc count grows with n. Refutes: eps at chance yet hinges <= 2x spline and SAE m <= 16 reaches 99%; harmonic gap equals the null. Kill: logistic eps >= 90% in all seeds (sign irrep learned) -> skip to torus/Z_360.

**Reuse and new code.** `model.py`, `probe.py`, `summarize_probes.py`, `viz_resid.py`, KANLayer, shared trainer. New: `groups.py` (~250), `group_data.py` (~120), null-control script (~80), `sae_tuning.py` (shared), spline autoencoder (~60).

**Effort and risks.** 2 days for null + pilot; +3 days for Z_360 and partial-product probes if the kill criterion is not triggered; ~20 GPU-hours. Risks: shortcut computation at the final position only; one-hot codes when d >= |G|; finite-set tiling being the correct SAE answer.

## Rung 3 — Stack of circles: modular arithmetic with a stack trace and bindings (distance to language: 2.5)

**Generator and tokenisation.** Expressions `E -> (E op E) | n | v` over Z_97, serialised in postfix; op sets staged: {+, -} first, x over Z_97^* (operands 1..96, discrete-log map checked against a brute-force table), then mixed. Leaf probability 0.4, depth <= 3 (24 tree shapes; hold out 4-5 named shapes), 3-8 operands, 0-3 LET bindings over 16 names. STACK-TRACE format: after every program token the model emits the current top-of-stack value (loss at every position), and query tokens ?2 / ?3 (p = 0.25) demand the second/third stack entries, so the deep stack must be materialised. Leaf-reuse variant: leaves may reference earlier trace results by index, so operands cannot be re-fetched from the prompt. Block 64, vocab ~130. 5-10M programs pre-generated to `/var/tmp` from 20k skeletons (20% held out) with held-out constant tuples; evaluator cross-checked against Python on 100k expressions.

**Language/reasoning aspect.** Recursion (nested sub-expressions on a stack), composition, variable binding and dereferencing, and a synthetic chain of thought in which intermediate results stay alive across sub-trees: the case with no analogue in the path models. Honestly: modular arithmetic inside a Dyck wrapper, no ambiguity.

**Ground-truth feature.** Pending operands at levels 1-3 as (cos, sin)(2 pi f v/97) at the model's own frequencies f (from the FFT of the digit embeddings; if the code is lookup-like, switch to CE targets and make no R² spline claims): a torus bound to depth. The pending result at the consuming operator, at `resid_mid` where both operands coexist: cos(2 pi (v1+v2)/97) is bilinear in two stored circles, exact with two cubic splines per product, O(1/eps) hinges. Non-linearity is forced by capacity: 3 levels x F frequencies x 2 dims + 16 names must exceed d, so d in {32, 64} deny binding by disjoint subspaces; a linear capacity check (ridge per level and frequency) is reported first, and ridge >= 0.95 everywhere is a null, not a refutation.

**Training task and quality bar.** 4L x d {32, 64, 128}, 2 seeds, batch 512, plateau-checked, 10-45 min per run. Bar: exact top-of-stack trace >= 99.5% on held-out skeletons and constants; ?2/?3 accuracy >= 99.5%; held-out shapes >= 98%; swapping one deep constant changes the answer as the simulator predicts on >= 99%; per-operand-count accuracy reported; depth-4 reported on length-matched trees only.

**Probes and SAE-specific checks.** >= 300k tokens, >= 20k per (level x holding-time) stratum. Targets: T1 top-of-stack circle at all k (one multi-output probe, per-dimension R²); T2 second-of-stack at the dominant frequency (headline); T3 third; T4 pending result at `resid_mid` of the consuming block, T4' post-block (control); T5 depth/operator (CE, position-only baseline); T6 97-way class (spline output 2-8 channels + linear head to match ReLU output cost); T7 bound variables. SAE: circle tiling per level (N_arc(m)); conjunctive splitting on the level-1 x level-2 torus (n_arc x D vs n_arc^D); absorption of "value = v" by "value = v at level 2" as m grows 64 -> 2048; binding-role confusion; Engels-style 2-D cluster recovery.

**Predictions and falsification.** Confirms: in a cell with present, non-linear T2, spline1L >= 0.9 at <= 1k params, hinges >= 2.5k, SAE + ridge >= 50k; T4 at `resid_mid`: spline m=4 >= 0.9 vs hinge m >= 16 vs SAE m >= 256, in excess of the null; N_arc grows linearly in m while SAE-ridge R² on T2 saturates. Refutes: hinges within 0.02 of splines at every budget <= 10k on T2 and SAE within 10x. Premise refuted: T4 near 0 for every family while T4' is linear (no bilinear feature ever materialised).

**Reuse and new code.** `model.py` (+hook), shared trainer, `paths_data` pattern, `probe.py`, `summarize_probes.py`, `probe_strata` logic, KANLayer, `sae_tuning.py`. New: generator + stack simulator + tracer + splits + cross-check (~300), `collect_trace` (~80), conjunction/absorption metrics (~100).

**Effort and risks.** 6-7 days for the first pass; the scratchpad-free RPN variant (+3 days) only if the deep-stack feature is present and non-linear. Risks: operands fetched by attention (leaf-reuse mitigates; measurable); lookup codes at p=97 with mixed ops (staged op sets); zero in the multiplicative circle (excluded).

## Rung 4 — Hidden-tree taxonomy with rank-constrained embeddings: hyperbolic vs simplicial codes and exact absorption (distance to language: 3)

**Generator and tokenisation.** A full 3-ary tree of depth 7 (3,280 nodes, 1,093 internal) as the clean absorption ground truth and a Galton-Watson tree (branching 1-5, ~3k nodes) as the primary tree for the curvature question, both with exact tree metric and Sarkar Poincaré coordinates in float64 (stored separately from uniform-edge scoring coordinates). Routing format = the path-transformer format with a length prefix so tree distance must be computed: `BOS s SEP g SEP L SEP n0..nL EOS` (L <= 14, block 22); the npz mirrors `gen_paths.py`. Flat node tokens plus a `hier` variant (node = its digit string, vocab ~25) as the embedding-table control. The decisive lever: a rank-k factorised token embedding, k in {2, 3, 4, 8, 16, 32}; at k = 2-3 a 3,280-node tree cannot be a 21-dim digit simplex, so a model that still meets the bar carries a curved low-dimensional node code by construction. Splits: random pairs; held-out LCAs at fixed depth (3 of 9 depth-2 nodes, ~2.5% of pairs; a depth-4 variant); 10% of nodes held out as goals entirely. The query format (is-a, ancestor, analogy) is deferred: its compose-only bar is a known negative result.

**Language/reasoning aspect.** Hierarchy and taxonomies (is-a chains), routing to the lowest common ancestor as "the shared category of two items", and an exact ground truth for the hierarchical-orthogonality picture the repo's `orthogonal_concepts` project studies in LLMs.

**Ground-truth feature.** Three exclusive codes: hyperbolic (depth = norm, tree distance = arccosh of a rational function, hierarchically refined angles), hierarchical simplices (ancestor digits in orthogonal level subspaces; distance = prefix match, piecewise-linear and hinge-native; the ideal sparse dictionary is the 1,093 subtree indicators, so feature absorption has an exact ground truth), or a linear depth line. Unconstrained embeddings are expected to give the digit code (curvature is not forced by routing); rank-k forces it.

**Training task and quality bar.** 4L d=128 and 3L d=64 per k, plateau training, ~20 min each, ~20 runs. Bar: valid simple path (no repeated node) and goal reached >= 99.5% on random pairs; >= 98% on the held-out-LCA split; >= 95% on held-out goal nodes; length prefix >= 99%; report the smallest k meeting the bar.

**Probes and SAE-specific checks.** Node-held-out probe split (20% of nodes as current node, depth-stratified) alongside the sequence split; the gap is the memorisation component. Step A, probe-independent: distortion of tree distance under Euclidean vs hyperbolic distance on k-D linear projections (k = 2, 3, 8), delta-hyperbolicity, norm-vs-depth test, whitened Park-style orthogonality. Step B, only where A finds curvature: layout-invariant targets (remaining hops, LCA depth, tree distance), Sarkar coordinates via the log-map, ancestor digits (CE, balanced accuracy); families include spline2L k=2 initialised from the linear projection and a 2-D hyperbolic probe matched against it. SAE headline: top-k SAEs with m in {64..1024} << n_internal and k in {8..64}; firing sets matched to subtree sets by Jaccard at fixed quantiles, per role (current vs goal); absorption rate = fraction of subtree(v) tokens where the parent feature is silent while a child-subtree feature fires, as curves against m/n_internal; digit-dictionary null; per-node dictionary (m >= n_nodes) as the degenerate endpoint; layer 0 reported separately as lookup.

**Predictions and falsification.** k=32: digit code (ridge >= 99% on all digits on unseen nodes, Euclidean distortion <= hyperbolic, splines never > 0.02 above hinges): expected, and not a refutation. Smallest passing k: 2-D hyperbolic distortion < 0.1 vs Euclidean > 0.3 and ridge <= 0.6 on Sarkar coordinates; confirms if cubic probes beat hinges by >= 0.05 at every budget 250-5k on layout-invariant targets and SAE + ridge needs >= 4x; refutes if hinges tie there. Absorption: under the digit code >= 70% subtree features with absorption rising with m and level; under the curved code the subtree Jaccard collapses into arc-like disk tiles.

**Reuse and new code.** `train_path.py`, `PathData` flat, `probe.py`, `summarize_probes.py`, `gen_paths` npz layout, KANLayer. New: `gen_tree.py` (~120), rank-k flag (~10), targets (~60), CE/balanced-accuracy path (~50), hyperbolic probe (~40), distortion/absorption analysis (~200).

**Effort and risks.** 4 days for routing with the rank sweep; queries later (5-6 days) if wanted. Risks: no k below 8 meets the bar (report as untested); float32 degeneracy of leaf coordinates (float64, log-map); top-k forcing absorption (sweep k).

## Rung 5 — Scoped belief grammar: nested scopes with per-scope hidden states and agreement-dependent closers (distance to language: 3.5)

**Generator and tokenisation.** Vocabulary `( ) [ ] A B C` + BOS/EOS/PAD. Nested scopes of bounded depth K = 3 (honestly a regular language; a K = 6 variant later), each carrying an independent Mess3 process (alpha = 0.85, x = 0.05; a slower 0.65/0.15 variant): at depth k, open with p_open = 0.05 (0 at K), close with p_close = 0.05 (0 at depth 0), otherwise emit a content token from h_k; a minimum content run of 4 before any bracket (bracket probabilities depend only on the count since the last bracket, so the factorisation and its unit test survive); lengths 128-192 so beliefs are beliefs (with the original 0.15/0.15 scopes had a median of 2 content tokens). The curved feature by construction: the closer type is drawn with P(`]` | h_{k-1} = h_k) = 0.9 and 0.1 otherwise, so agreement between parent and child hidden states, not the opener, decides the closer; a coupled variant redraws the parent state on close from an XOR-like table P(h' | h_p, h_c). Exact oracle: the joint pushdown-HMM forward filter (<= 777 states) with the end-of-sequence closing policy inside the transition matrix, so every conditional is exact; the factorised computation is a unit test only. 2M sequences on the fly.

**Language/reasoning aspect.** Embedded clauses whose outer latent (speaker, tense, topic) must be resumed after the inner clause closes, long-distance agreement across an embedded clause (Linzen-style), and uncertainty tracking over several latents at once: the PCFG extension of the repo's belief-state project.

**Ground-truth feature.** Each per-level belief b_j on the Mess3 fractal is expected linear (Shai et al.). The agreement scalar a = sum_i b_{k-1}(i) b_k(i) and the pair-joint b_{k-1} (x) b_k (9-D) are quadratic forms of two linearly stored beliefs, exact with two cubic-spline units per product term and only approximable by hinges, and the model must compute a at every closable position. The addressing question (absolute padded stack vs relative top/parent vs the depth-k block of the joint belief) is reported as "which coordinate system is linear", never as non-linearity; all targets in probability and log coordinates.

**Training task and quality bar.** 4L x d {32, 64, 128}, 2 seeds, plateau on held-out KL. Bar: content-only KL(oracle || model) <= 0.01 nats, stratified by depth and tokens-since-open and reported as a fraction of the stationary-belief baseline; <= 0.02 at the first content token after each close per depth; closer distribution within 0.02 nats (agreement learned); the same-level k-gram ceiling (k = 1..8) reported and beaten.

**Probes and SAE-specific checks.** Timing gate first: pooled and per-depth ridge for b_parent inside child scopes (< 0.5 everywhere = lazy recompute; then probe at the closer and one token after) plus a causal patch of parent-level content tokens. Targets at closable positions: a, pair-joint, b_top, b_parent, post-close b_p'. Splines with G in {8, 16, 32}, degree-1 and MLP2 controls. SAE widths {128, 512, 2048} x k {1, 4, 16}: features labelled by token address (IFS images overlap ~3%), footprint on the simplex, features in the belief subspace vs width, k=1 (must tile) vs k=3 (can be linear); exclude the first 8 and last 4 positions, stratify by position bin.

**Predictions and falsification.** Replication: b_top ridge >= 0.95 from layer 2. Confirms: ridge on a and the pair-joint <= 0.6 in both coordinate systems while spline1L >= 0.9 at <= 2.5k params, ReLU needs >= 3x, SAE >= 10x. Refutes: ReLU within 0.02 at <= 2.5k and SAE within 3x. Void: a is linear (computed early and stored as a direction), reported as such.

**Reuse and new code.** Notebook helpers lifted into `hmm.py` (~0.5 day), `model.py`, shared trainer with all-token loss and oracle KL (shared with Rung 7), `probe.py`, `summarize_probes.py`, simplex plots. New: `gen_scoped_mess3.py` with joint filter and brute-force test (~250), collect (~80), simplex footprint analysis (~150).

**Effort and risks.** 7-8 days. Risks: KL bar unreachable at d=64 (pre-registered fallback: <= 0.02 with a visible fractal); lazy recompute (long scopes, gate); the notebook never reached a crisp fractal (AdamW, 4 layers, block 192).

## Rung 6 — Clock-world relational deduction: binding names to angles across hops (distance to language: 3.5)

**Generator and tokenisation.** Episodes with E in {4..8} entities named from 64 name tokens, hidden angles on Z_36. One anchor fact `R at k` fixes the gauge; the remaining facts are the edges of a Prüfer-sampled uniform spanning tree rendered `X cw k Y` or `Y ccw k X` (p = 0.5) in random order. One query per episode (later queries are derivable from earlier answers): relative `? X Y = k` or absolute `? X = k`, hop count drawn from the tree's realised distance distribution and reported. Block 64; vocab 64 + 36 + 9. 1% of name pairs held out as query pairs only (episode rejection < 5%). Optional second grammar: dihedral D_18 relations (non-abelian). 2M episodes on the fly, 40k held out.

**Language/reasoning aspect.** Variable binding across a context, multi-hop deduction along a unique chain of premises, relation canonicalisation and set-like premises: bAbI task 19 with a continuous group. No syntax or recursion; canonicalisation is a fixed two-token map.

**Ground-truth feature.** Absolute angles theta_X, theta_Y (trained through the absolute queries, so they are model targets rather than a convention) in the model's own Fourier frame, read off the DFT of the 36 offset-token embedding rows (F frequencies holding > 90% of energy). Delta = theta_X - theta_Y is bilinear: cos Delta = cos tX cos tY + sin tX sin tY. The factorised stage (operands linear, Delta not) lives post-attention, pre-MLP and is position-specific (X token, Y token, `=` token), so both hooks and all three positions are probed. Honest expectation: a 4-8x parameter gap on a quadratic form (m = 4 splines ~600 params vs ~32 hinges ~4.2k), not orders of magnitude.

**Training task and quality bar.** Sweep 2-4 layers x d {64, 128} to find the smallest model meeting the bar (primary probe target; 6L d=128 if 5-hop stalls); 3 seeds; plateau on validation exact answer (cap 200k steps). Bar: exact answer >= 99.5% overall and >= 99% per hop stratum 1..5; >= 99.5% on held-out name pairs; consistency (X,Y)+(Y,Z) = (X,Z) >= 99.5% across separate episodes sharing a tree; a model trained on canonical BFS fact order tested on shuffled order (non-vacuous invariance).

**Probes and SAE-specific checks.** Hooks: every block boundary and every `resid_mid`; positions X, Y, `=`; targets: 2F-dim Fourier vectors of theta_X, theta_Y, Delta plus 36-way linear softmax accuracy (basis-free), hop count, a causal "resolvable" flag at fact tokens. Families: ridge, SAE + ridge (m 64-1024), ReLU m {2..128}, spline1L m {2..64}, degree-1, MLP2; matched grid {0.5k, 1k, 2.5k, 5k, 10k}; >= 40k episodes; the SAE trained on the same position-restricted tokens (and on all positions, reported separately). Input-space control: synthetic (cos tX, sin tX, cos tY, sin tY) -> (cos Delta, sin Delta) in random 128-D directions with noise. Discriminating SAE test: SAE + ridge R² for Delta vs m at the factorised hook, the same SAE evaluated on the operands (operands read at small m; Delta stays at the linear baseline until conjunction features appear); name/arc MI as a side table only.

**Predictions and falsification.** Confirms: a (position, hook) cell exists with 36-way linear accuracy >= 0.95 on both operands and <= 0.5 on Delta; there spline1L m = 4-8 reaches >= 0.9 while hinges need m >= 32 and SAE + ridge m >= 512, with the gap within 30% of the input-space control. Refutes: at that cell ReLU m <= 8 (<= 2x params) reaches the same R² and SAE + ridge matches at m <= 64. Null: no such cell (Delta linear at the first hook where it is decodable): the model chains offsets directly; reported, not counted.

**Reuse and new code.** `model.py` (+hook), shared trainer, `probe.py`, `summarize_probes.py`, `viz_resid.py`, `sae_tuning.py`. New: `clockworld.py` (~250), FFT basis extraction (~50), collect (~100).

**Effort and risks.** 8 days (generator 1, hooks/basis 0.5, training 1.5, probes 1.5, SAE curve 1, controls and write-up 1.5, slack 1). Risks: the 5-hop bar with random fact order; the factorised stage one layer thick; multi-frequency codes (basis-free accuracy handles it).

## Rung 7 — In-context Bayesian chains with a latent hyperparameter: inferring how much to trust the context (distance to language: 4)

**Generator and tokenisation.** S = 6 symbols, length 256. Per sequence, transition rows T_s ~ Dirichlet(alpha 1) with the concentration alpha itself latent, drawn from {0.1, 1, 10} (hierarchical prior). Control: fixed alpha in {1, 3}. Optional treatment 2: change-point chains (T redrawn at a geometric change point, hazard 1/64) with the exact Adams-MacKay run-length posterior. Exact predictor for the hierarchical case: a mixture over alpha with weights w_alpha proportional to the Dirichlet-multinomial marginal likelihood of all rows' counts (closed-form lgamma sums) times (alpha + n_{s,x})/(S alpha + n_s); unit-tested against brute-force marginals on short sequences. The hidden-state variant is dropped (no exact posterior).

**Language/reasoning aspect.** In-context learning of statistics, the statistical-induction-head regime that underlies LM adaptation, plus inference about the rules (is this context reliable, how much to smooth) rather than only the state: the analogue of learning a new word's distribution from context and deciding how far to trust it.

**Ground-truth feature.** The critics' reversal is adopted as the prediction: the posterior mean (alpha + n)/(S alpha + n_s) is *linear*, produced directly by an averaging head with a BOS sink (Edelman et al. 2024; Nichani et al. 2024), and the softmax absorbs the denominator; raw counts and the sink fraction alpha_0/(alpha_0 + n_s) are the non-linear quantities, but they are 1-D and cheap (4 hinges fit them to R² 0.99). The engineered feature is w_alpha, a non-linear function of every row's counts, and the mixed predictive sum_alpha w_alpha x ratio_alpha, product-structured and multi-dimensional, which the output softmax cannot absorb and one head cannot produce. The run-length posterior is a multi-dimensional non-linear belief over time-since-change.

**Training task and quality bar.** L in {2, 3, 4} x d in {32, 64, 128}, trained to plateau on the Bayes gap; probe the smallest passing model and one over-parametrised one. Bar, per position bucket from 32: (KL_model - KL_bayes)/(KL_unigram - KL_bayes) <= 0.05 against the exact hierarchical predictor; the model beats MLE plug-in and last-occurrence copy at positions 8-64; the model's implied alpha (fit by minimising KL to the add-alpha family per sequence) classifies the generating alpha at >= 90% by position 64, proof that the hyperparameter is inferred.

**Probes and SAE-specific checks.** Within position bins ([40,56], [128,144], [224,240]) or with t partialled out; pre-LN and post-LN residual and per-head outputs. Targets: w_alpha (3-D, CE and R²); current-row posterior mean with an identity-output probe (are frequencies linear) and a softmax-output probe (are log-numerators linear); n_s and the sink fraction (ceiling from the 4-hinge fit); the mixed predictive scored as excess KL to Bayes. Budgets recomputed per target including the output layer. Mechanism check: attention patterns for a uniform-over-matching-positions head with a BOS sink. SAE: do features track counts, ratios or alpha; count-bin splitting along the 1/(c + n) curve; whether w_alpha x ratio conjunctions appear as m grows.

**Predictions and falsification.** Control: ridge on the posterior mean >= 0.95 in-bin at layer 2 (expected; it refutes the original "ratios are non-linear" premise, not the hypothesis). Treatment confirms: w_alpha non-linear (ridge <= 0.6 within bin) and spline1L reaches excess KL <= 0.01 on the mixed predictive at <= 2k params while hinges need >= 3x and SAE + ridge >= 10x. Refutes: hinges within 0.02 at every budget <= 2k and SAE within 3x. Void: w_alpha linear from layer 2 (the hyperparameter posterior stored as a direction), itself a finding about ICL.

**Reuse and new code.** `model.py`, shared trainer with all-token loss and KL evaluator (Rung 5), `probe.py`, `summarize_probes.py`, the notebook's KL-callback pattern. New: `dirichlet_data.py` (generator, conjugate/hierarchical/run-length predictors, unit tests, baseline ladder; ~250), per-position collector (~80).

**Effort and risks.** 5-6 days for control + hierarchical treatment; +1.5 for change-point. Risks: alpha = 0.1 sequences solved by copying (per-alpha reporting); position confound (bins); LayerNorm linearising the sink fraction (both hooks).

## Suggested order and what each rung buys

1. **Rung 2 (group tracking) first, 2-day pilot.** It is the cheapest, it builds the null-control script, `sae_tuning.py`, the resid_mid hook and the probe upgrades every other rung needs, and it asks the sharpest question: on a verified, exactly known curved code (Chebyshev harmonics of a stored circle, the flip bit if it survives the null), do cubic readouts beat hinges by more than the probe-side ceiling? A negative here means the input-space advantage does not transfer to low-dimensional curved codes in activation space at all; the remaining rungs then test only product/multi-dimensional features and the SAE failure modes (splitting, absorption, conjunction cost), which stay valuable regardless.
2. **Rung 1 (winding classes).** Reuses the existing path models as its positive control and takes ~5 days. A negative (direct-sum circle + counter, helix gap equal to the free-model chart gap) means "circle x counter" is not a curved feature and the deliverable is the SAE factorisation count.
3. **Rung 6 (clock-world)** before **Rung 3 (stack of circles)**: both test bilinear composition at the post-attention state, but clock-world forces the operands to be linear targets through the absolute queries and has the cleaner language flavour. A negative at rung 6 (hinges tie on Delta at the factorised cell) kills the "product of two circles" branch of the hypothesis; rung 3 then serves the binding/absorption SAE questions and the deep-stack presence test rather than the spline claim.
4. **Rung 5 (scoped belief grammar).** The only rung where the curved feature is a quadratic form of two *belief* vectors that the task forces at every closable position. A negative here (agreement scalar linear, or hinges tie) ends the belief-product branch; a positive is the first spline win on a feature with a real linguistic reading.
5. **Rung 4 (rank-constrained tree).** Independent of the others; its absorption ground truth is an SAE result on its own, and the rank-k sweep is the only design in the set where curvature can be dialled in. A negative at the smallest passing k means splines do not exploit even an engineered hyperbolic node code.
6. **Rung 7 (hierarchical in-context chains).** Last, because its control is expected to *refute* the naive ratio premise and only the hyperparameter posterior can carry a product feature; a negative says ICL statistics are linear all the way down, which bounds the hypothesis's relevance to in-context learning.

Across the ladder, the hypothesis survives only if at least one rung produces a pre-registered cell where presence and non-linearity hold, the cubic probe beats hinges by more than the null-control ceiling and the seed spread, and the SAE needs an order of magnitude more parameters. If rungs 2, 6 and 5 all come out negative, the honest conclusion is that spline order matters for *modelling* curved boundaries in input space but not for *reading out* the codes small transformers build, and the programme should pivot to the SAE-specific results (splitting of circles, conjunction cost of products, absorption in hierarchies), which every rung still delivers.

## Dropped ideas and why

- **Egocentric navigation on S² vs T².** Curvature is quantised away at the proposed resolution (heading changes on 2.8% of states, 90% of them near the poles); SO(3) embeds linearly as a 9-D frame matrix; the S²/T² contrast confounds gauge, group dimension and grid. Non-abelian composition is covered by Rung 2.
- **Categorical causal patterns on a discrete torus.** Any function of a k-valued categorical is linear in its one-hot; every (value, shift) combination is seen, so no Fourier code is induced. Rungs 2 and 3 with held-out combinations cover the cyclic case.
- **Dead reckoning on the torus surfaces.** The M = 64 codebook does not exist (26 lattice moves); ring concentration is constant at 0.996; the posterior mean is a linear functional of the belief. A hidden-start localisation variant with next-move loss could be attached to Rung 1 later if belief geometry on the shapes is wanted.
- **Agreement-Dyck and turtle-Dyck.** The open schedule contradicts its own depth bound and the length filter keeps 9.7% of sequences; stack content is fetched by attention (Yao et al.), and the depth gate is hinge-native. The interior-dependent-closer fix converges with Rungs 3 and 5.
- **Letter-counter product-automaton geodesics.** Per-cell letters bind on 7-8% of triples with 2-cycle exploits; rescued as Rung 1's oriented-wall cocycle. Bounded Dyck and keys-and-doors variants dropped (regular; hinge-shaped by design).
- **Parallel Mess3 product beliefs (Segre variety).** The product is additive in log coordinates at pair positions and forced only in the shared-alphabet variant; that variant can be added to Rung 5 as a binding-ambiguity extension.
- **Taxonomy query format and SCAN-style instruction stage.** The compose-only bar is a known negative result (Wang et al. 2024) and the SCAN splits are routinely failed; both deferred until a routing/winding rung shows a curved code worth composing over.
